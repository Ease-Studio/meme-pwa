📱 How to Install on iOS

  1. 1️⃣Open Safari Browser
    Yes, must be Safari (other browsers don't support this on iOS).
  2. 2️⃣Visit this website using Safari: https://ease-studio.github.io/meme-pwa/
  3. 3️⃣Tap the Share Button
    It's the square with an up arrow at the bottom of the screen.
  4. 4️⃣Select “Add to Home Screen”
    Scroll down in the share menu and tap this option.
  5. 5️⃣Confirm
    Rename if you want, then tap Add in the top-right corner.
  6. ✅Done!
    The app icon will now appear on your home screen, ready to use!
