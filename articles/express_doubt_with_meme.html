How to express Doubt by memes

Stop Lying to yourself

Meaning: Tell someone to not believe in their thoughts

When to Use

When someone is clearly ignoring reality

When someone is in denial about an obvious outcome

When someone is overestimating themselves in a humorous way

Tone

⚠️ Medium risk — can sound harsh if used seriously
✅ Works best with friends or self-jokes

Example Situations

Friend says: “I can finish this project in 10 minutes.”

Someone says: “I don’t need to study, I’ll pass easily.”

Story Example

You and your friend are playing a game. They keep losing but say “Next round I’ll win easily.”
→ Send this meme to jokingly call out overconfidence.

“Press X to Doubt”

Meaning: Strong disbelief on something

When to Use

When something sounds fake

When claims sound exaggerated

When you want to disagree without arguing

Tone

✅ Funny
✅ Widely understood online
✅ Safe for most situations

Example Situations

“I made $1 million in one day from crypto.”

“This app can make you fluent in English overnight.”

Story Example

Someone posts “Guaranteed 100% success method.”
→ Comment with this meme to show skepticism without typing a long argument.

You expect me to believe that?

Meaning: “You expect me to believe that?”

When to Use

When a story sounds very unrealistic

When someone gives an obvious excuse

When you want dramatic reaction

Tone

⚠️ Slightly confrontational
✅ Good for jokes with friends

Example Situations

“My dog ate my homework.”

“I didn’t reply because my phone was broken… for 3 days.”

Story Example

Friend shows up late and says traffic was empty but they still arrived late.
→ Send this meme.

Pinocchio Nose Growing

Meaning: Point out someone's lies.

When to Use

When someone tells obvious lies

When exaggeration is obvious

When jokingly calling out dishonesty

Tone

⚠️ Can feel like direct accusation
✅ Use mostly in playful contexts

Example Situations

“I totally didn’t eat your snacks.”

“I studied all night.” (but they were gaming)

Story Example

Your friend says they didn’t check their phone, but you saw them online.
→ Send this meme as playful callout.

Kermit “But That’s None of My Business”

Meaning: Heard a lie but does not care ("But That’s None of My Business")

When to Use

When observing drama

When you notice lies but want to stay neutral

When making indirect commentary

Tone

✅ Safe
✅ Passive-aggressive humor
✅ Social media friendly

Example Situations

Someone posts motivational quotes but behaves opposite in real life

Someone criticizes others for things they also do

Story Example

Coworker complains about lazy people… but never finishes tasks.
→ Post this meme.

Side Eye / Suspicious Look Memes

Meaning: Silently disbelieve on something, knowing the outcome but do not dare to say

When to Use

When you suspect something is wrong

When you don’t want open conflict

When you want subtle humor

Tone

✅ Low conflict
✅ Very flexible

Example Situations

Someone says “Trust me bro.”

Someone says “This will only take 5 minutes.”

Story Example

Your friend says they will “just play one game” but usually plays for 3 hours.
→ Send side eye meme.

“Trust with every cell in body”

Meaning: Mocking fake claims with no proof.

When to Use

When someone claims expertise without proof

When something sounds too confident

When mocking fake “expert advice”

Tone

✅ Sarcastic
✅ Popular in internet culture

Example Situations

“This investment is guaranteed profit.”

“I watched one tutorial, now I’m an expert.”

Story Example

Someone says they can fix your computer after watching one video.
→ Send this meme.
