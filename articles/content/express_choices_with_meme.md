# How to express choices by memes

## Two Buttons Meme

**Meaning:** Hard choice between 2 options.

Use when: Both choices are tempting or both are bad.

![Two Buttons meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/5.jpg?generation=1746452427460423&alt=media)

**When to use:**

* You must choose between two good options
* You must choose between two bad options
* Trade-off situations
* Deadline or pressure decisions

**Example:**

* “Sleep early” vs “Watch one more episode.”
* “Ship fast” vs “Ship bug-free.”

**Tone:**

* Relatable
* Light stress humor
* Dramatic but funny
* Everyday life comedy

## Drake Hotline Bling

**Meaning:** Reject one choice, approve another.

![Drake Hotline Bling meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/0.jpg?generation=1746000648523347&alt=media)

**When to use:**

* Clear preference
* Comparing old way vs better way
* Showing upgrade / improvement
* Simple decision messaging

**Example:**

* ❌ Writing manual SQL

* ✅ Using ORM

* ❌ 10 meetings

* ✅ 1 clear document

**Tone:**

* Confident
* Clean humor
* Slightly smug but friendly
* Very internet-friendly

## Galaxy Brain (Choice Evolution)

**Meaning:** Showing “levels” of choices from basic → genius → absurd.

![Galaxy Brain](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/353.jpg?generation=1746453394922635&alt=media)

**When to use:**

* Showing progression of ideas
* Comparing beginner → expert thinking
* Ironically showing “overthinking”
* Making fun of extreme solutions

**Example:**

* Junior: Fix bug manually
* Mid: Write test
* Senior: Prevent bug class
* Galaxy: Delete feature

**Tone:**

* Nerd humor
* Satirical intelligence
* Escalating absurdity
* Tech / internet culture

---

## Distracted Boyfriend

**Meaning:** Leaving old choice for new tempting choice.

![Distracted Boyfriend](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/3.jpg?generation=1746452423819012&alt=media)

**When to use:**

* Shiny new technology temptation
* Losing focus from original plan
* Feature creep
* Trend chasing

**Example:**

* Boyfriend: “Me”
* Girlfriend: “Current project”
* Other girl: “New framework released yesterday”

**Tone:**

* Self-aware humor
* Slightly critical
* Relatable dev / work life joke
* Light roast

## “This Is Fine” Dog

**Meaning:** Choosing to ignore problem / knowingly making bad choice.

![This is Fine meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/563.jpg?generation=1746453724376444&alt=media)

**When to use:**

* Accepting bad situation
* Ignoring warning signs
* “We’ll deal with it later” decisions
* Technical debt moments

**Example:**

* “Production error rate rising.” → This is fine.
* “Security patch overdue.” → This is fine.

**Tone:**

* Dark humor (but light presentation)
* Resigned acceptance
* Self-aware
* Calm chaos energy
