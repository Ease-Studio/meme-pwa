# How to express frustration by memes
---
## Facepalm

**Meaning:** Someone did something so stupid or obvious that it’s painful to watch.

![Facepalm meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/174.jpg?generation=1746453070965183&alt=media)

**When to use:**

* Obvious mistake happens
* Someone ignores clear instructions
* Repeating the same mistake again
* Watching preventable failure happen

**Example:**

* “Documentation literally says DO NOT DELETE.”
* “User enters password in username field.”

**Tone:**

* Mild frustration
* Disappointed humor
* Reaction-based
* Not aggressive

---


## Table Flip

**Meaning:** Extreme frustration → giving up.

![Table Flip meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/1054.jpg?generation=1746454658441113&alt=media)

**When to use:**

* After trying many times and still failing
* When system completely breaks
* When rules make no sense
* When patience runs out

**Example:**

* “Build fails for unknown reason.”
* “Fix one bug → three new bugs appear.”

**Tone:**

* Dramatic frustration
* Comedy rage
* Overreaction humor
* Chaotic energy

## This Is Fine (Frustration Version)

**Meaning:** You are frustrated but pretending everything is okay.

![This is Fine meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/2437.jpg?generation=1746456612808773&alt=media)

**When to use:**

* Problems stacking up
* Stress you can’t avoid
* Quiet suffering
* Forced professionalism

**Example:**

* “Production errors increasing but deadline stays.”
* “Three urgent tasks added at once.”

**Tone:**

* Quiet frustration
* Dark humor (light delivery)
* Resigned acceptance
* Relatable work stress

---

## Scream Internally / Calm Outside

**Meaning:** Very frustrated inside but staying polite outside.

![Internal Screaming meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/10902.jpg?generation=1746541625101399&alt=media)

**When to use:**

* Customer says something unreasonable
* Boss changes requirements last minute
* Group project chaos
* Social situations where you must stay calm

**Example:**

* “Can you redo everything but keep same deadline?”
* “Client changes scope after approval.”

**Tone:**

* Professional frustration
* Socially controlled anger
* Relatable workplace humor
* Self-control comedy

## Repeated Button Press

**Meaning:** Frustration when something simple refuses to work.

![Button Spam meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/71.jpg?generation=1746452770185189&alt=media)

**When to use:**

* Slow loading system
* Broken UI button
* Laggy apps
* Waiting for response

**Example:**

* “Click login → nothing happens.”
* “Refresh page → still broken.”

**Tone:**

* Everyday frustration
* Relatable tech humor
* Light annoyance
* Casual internet tone
