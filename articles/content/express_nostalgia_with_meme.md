# How to express nostalgia by memes

## Wojak Nostalgia

Meaning: Missing old times / remembering past era
Use when: Talking about childhood, old internet, or “good old days”

![Wojak nostalgia meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/2724.jpg?generation=1746457099762638&alt=media)

Often connected with **SpongeBob SquarePants** childhood memories.

---

## Toy Story “Andy Growing Up” Feeling

Meaning: Realizing time has passed / childhood is over
Use when: Old game shutting down, friends drifting apart, growing up moments

![Toy Story nostalgia meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/2686.jpg?generation=1746457033943159&alt=media)

Often associated with emotional scenes from **Toy Story 3**.

---

## “Remember This Guy ?” Meme

Meaning: Shared memory with people from same generation
Use when: Old TV shows, old school snacks, old apps, old school life

![Remember This Guy ? meme](https://storage.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/y_meme_templates/o/13814.jpg?generation=1746623944484230&alt=media)

---

## “Back In My Day” Meme

Meaning: Comparing past vs present (usually joking)
Use when: Technology, school life, gaming, social media differences
